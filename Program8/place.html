My Favourite place Munnar

Munnar is one of the most beautiful hill stations in kerala. It is famous for its green tea plantations ,mountains,waterfalls and Pleasant climat.

Munnar is a wonderful place to enjoy nature and spend time with family. Thebeautiful scenery and peaceful atmosphere make it my favourite place.
